# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive. No insults, harassment, or personal attacks.
- No spam, advertising, or self-promotion outside of relevant project discussion.
- Do not post other people's personal information.
- Do not use this project (or discussions around it) to distribute or promote harmful, illegal, or adult content.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, or code that violate this code of conduct, and may block users who are repeatedly or seriously in violation.
